# My project brief

## The question
Which rural settlements in the Chamwino district are disconnected from the secondary school catchment zones?

## The data I need
- District boundaries - geoBoundaries - https://www.geoboundaries.org - GeoJSON - 5 MB
- Schools - OSM via HDX - https://data.humdata.org - GeoPackage 6.4 MB
- Population - WorldPop - https://www.worldpop.org - 407 MB
- DEM - OpenTopology - https://www.opentopography.org - 83.7 MB
- Transport networks - Geofabrik - https://www.geofabrik.de
